Video Game: Emperor's Tundra

In Media Device Architecture (CS 2261), the final project was to create a Game Boy Advance game in C. I made an action adventure game centered around a penguin trying to save the world. The game can be played in the class Hall of Fame here or you can double click the image on the right!

Default controls are:

B: Z
A: X
Start: Enter
Select: Backspace
D-Pad: Arrow Keys
P: Pause Game and Edit Settings

Video Game: Behind the Show

In Interactive Narrative (LMC 4720), I and 2 other individuals were tasked to remix an existing media into a new interactive experience. Behind the Show occurs on the other side of The Truman Show, where the player acts as the showrunner and must navigate controlling Truman’s environment and relationships to please his vast audience and sponsors, exploring the morality of trapping someone in a false reality. The game was made using Twine, and I was responsible for coding most of the css, javascript, and branching path & different ending mechanics.

Click on the text options to play!

Video Game: Anchored Deep

For Intro to Game Studies (LMC 2410), I was in a group with 4 other individuals. We were to create a game around design pillars and a gameplay loop. The player can explore, find journal pieces, and fight ocean monsters. I coded the canonball mechanic, enemies, and healthbar system. This was created in Unity.

Video Game: Slidin' Penguins

For Intro to Game Studies (LMC 2410), I was tasked to make a simple game based around mechanics. My mechanic was physics/interaction. I demonstrated this through player movement, collision, and collecting and counting coins. This was created in Unity. Click on the screen to the right to play the game demo!

Default controls are:

W: Up
A: Left
S: Down
D: Right

Python Website: Study Spot

Using Django in Objects and Design (CS 2340), a group of 4 fellow students and I created a college website group study spot site. It allows users to create study meetups with many attributes such as amount of people, location, time, and type of study. It also allows users to join those groups, view study locations on a map, and create profiles.

Python Website: Georgia Tech Movie Store

Using Django in Objects and Design (CS 2340), a group of 4 fellow students and I created a movie store. It functions similarly to a real store, with reviews, the ability to purchase items, and create accounts. There is also admin functionality to create new movie listings and adjust prices.

Python Website: Breath of the Wild Compendium

Using public API and Streamlit in Intro to Computing (CS 1301), I was able to make a useful website that displays weapons, items, etc. and tell stats about them in The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild. I included 3 search features: by name, by item number, or randomly by category.

HTML Website: Harry Potter/Georgia Tech Sorting Quiz

I created a "Buzz Feed" style form in Intro to Computing (CS 1301) that would ask questions about Georgia Tech related things and sort the user into a Hogwarts house. I used different html form features and if statements to determine the house.

AI App: TimeSlips Mobile App

For Junior Capstone (CX 3803), a group of 4 students and I collaborated with the nonprofit TimeSlips to make an app to better help with Dementia care. This included easily accessible creative prompts, session creation and transcription, quick feedback, and more. I was primarily a UI/UX developer and designer for the project. AI Chatbot: Judicial AI

In a three person group with the help of AI in Intro to Computational Media (LMC 2700), we created a Judicial AI chatbot. The purpose is to speculate on a possible future of the legal system in the United States. The current iteration takes in files of United States general law, and gives advice based upon that information. It uses OpenAI to give accurate responses and communicate with the user.

Conversational AI Agent: Dialogflow CX

In Objects and Design (CS 2340), I made a AI agent using Google Cloud that helped describe Dialogflow CX. The AI agent could be called using a phone number and use voice prompts and suggestions to help the user.

Film: You Have 10 Minutes

In Video Production (LMC 2720), the final project was to write, record, and edit a short film. I produced a short film about a college student who suddenly finds himself saving his campus from a threat... and he only has 10 minutes to do so. I shot the film on a Blackmagic Pocket 6K G2 and edited it using DaVinci Resolve. The short film went on to win Best Editing for a Narrative Short at the LMCFilms Student Film Fest.

Film: MILLENNAIM
